ml-kit

The classic algorithms, from scratch — for offline features. One file, zero dependencies, no model, no API key, no network.

Boundary. This kit is for ranking, similarity, clustering and simple trend. It is not a diagnostic or predictive engine, and nothing in it may be used to claim a clinical or medical capability.

What's in it

  • tfidf + search — ranked text search
  • NaiveBayes — a tiny classifier
  • knn, kmeans, pca2 — similarity, clustering, plotting
  • id3 + predict — a small rules classifier
  • linearRegression — a trend line
  • markov + generate — statistical generation from a corpus
  • grammar — rule-based generation (rules, slots, weighted alternatives)

Usage and the boundary statement are in the README. Tests: node test-node.js.
